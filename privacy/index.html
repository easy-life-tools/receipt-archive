Privacy Policy

Receipt Archive · Last updated: November 24, 2026

In short: Receipt Archive does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your receipts stay on your device and in your own private iCloud.

What the app stores

The app stores the photos of receipts you take and the information you add to them (a star, a warranty period, an item name, a purchase date). This data is stored on your device.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your receipts are synced through Apple's iCloud to your private iCloud database, so they are available on your other devices. Only you can access this data. The developer has no access to it. iCloud is provided by Apple and is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn off iCloud for the app in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Camera

The camera is used only to take photos of receipts inside the app. Photos are not sent anywhere other than your private iCloud (if enabled).

Notifications

Warranty reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No data is sent to any server.

Sharing and PDF export

Receipts leave the app only when you choose to share them or export them to PDF, using the standard iOS share sheet (for example AirDrop, Messages or Mail).

Third parties

The app does not use any third-party services, SDKs, analytics or advertising.

Deleting your data

  • Delete individual receipts in the app.
  • Deleting the app removes all data stored on the device.
  • To delete data from iCloud, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Children

The app does not collect any data, including from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
