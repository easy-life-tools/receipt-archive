Receipt Archive

Snap receipts in seconds and keep track of warranties.

Questions

How do I take a photo quickly?
Add the Take Receipt Photo control to Control Center or to your Lock Screen. You can also assign it to the Action button (Settings → Action Button → Shortcut) or ask Siri: “Take a receipt photo in Receipts”.

Are my receipts synced between my devices?
Yes, when you are signed in to the same iCloud account on all devices. Only you can see your receipts.

How do warranty reminders work?
Open a receipt, tap the shield button and choose the warranty period. You will get a reminder 30 days before the warranty ends. All warranties are listed in the Warranties tab.

How do I export receipts?
Share a single receipt with the share button, select several receipts with ⋯ → Select, or create one PDF for a month or a date range with ⋯ → Export to PDF.

I deleted a receipt by mistake. Can I get it back?
No, deleting a receipt removes it from the device and from iCloud.
